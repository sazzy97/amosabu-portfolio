● B2B SaaS & SME Logistics • Zuri Logistics Case Study

SwiftLog Platform

Empowering small and medium-scale retailers across emerging markets with affordable on-demand parcel dispatch, real-time waypoint GPS tracking, and transparent courier communication.

Client Zuri
My Role Lead Product Designer
Timeline 7 Weeks, 3 Days
Platform iOS & Android App
01 / The Challenge

Traditional delivery services were draining SME margins and destroying customer trust.

For African e-commerce merchants and independent retailers, dispatching goods through regular post offices or informal commercial interstate transport was agonizingly slow and prohibitively expensive. Deliveries arrived damaged or days late, leaving customers frustrated and refusing repeat business.

01

Exorbitant Shipping Rates

Unregulated commercial drivers and post offices charged high fees that wiped out the profit margins of growing small businesses.

02

Black-Hole Transit Times

With zero live tracking, merchants had no way of knowing where a package was once handed over, leading to constant customer complaints.

03

Zero Customer Support

When packages were delayed or lost, merchants had no direct communication channel with couriers, directly harming their brand reputation.

02 / The Strategy

Empathetic Onboarding & Frictionless Setup

Establishing confidence on first launch by clearly highlighting Swift Delivery, Direct Driver Communication, and Real-Time Tracking.

Structured onboarding journey: Communicates transparent rates, built-in rider contact, and precision GPS tracking.

03 / End-to-End Workflow

From Package Creation to Doorstep Delivery

A 4-stage logistics pipeline designed to reduce dispatch booking from 15 minutes of chaotic phone calls down to under 60 seconds.

Stage 01: Package Configuration

Dynamic Category & Size Selectors

Replaces ambiguous freight categories with intuitive chips. Merchants pick their industry (Gadgets, Fashion, Electronics, Groceries), specify package vulnerability (Fragile, Shatter Resistant), and choose clear weight tiers (1-3kg, 3-6kg, 6-10kg).

  • ✓ Smart package handling tags (Fragile flags)
  • ✓ Transparent tiered pricing based on weight
  • ✓ Custom package description notes for riders
Stage 02: Transparent Settlement

Order Summary & Flat Rates

Provides a full, scannable order breakdown before payment. Both sender and receiver addresses (e.g. Egbeda to Lekki Phase 1) are displayed alongside package specifics and a guaranteed flat delivery fee (₦3,500), eliminating surprise courier surcharges.

  • ✓ Inline edit triggers for rapid address corrections
  • ✓ Verified recipient phone bridge
  • ✓ Flat ₦3,500 rate with 25% promo code support
Stage 03: Live In-Transit Visibility

Real-Time GPS Map & Rider Bridge

Merchants and end-buyers watch the parcel progress along live road maps (e.g., Agege to Ikeja). Features a persistent bottom driver bar identifying the courier (Obasogie Adeoye) with direct 1-tap call and in-app chat buttons.

  • ✓ Turn-by-turn waypoint progress visualizer
  • ✓ In-app voice call & message bridge to driver
  • ✓ Shareable web tracking link for end customers
Stage 04: Audit & Confirmation

Timestamped Milestone Audits

Detailed tracking under Order ID (#123987) with timestamped progress checkpoints: Order Confirmed → Order Processed → Out for Delivery → Delivered, paired with a permanent digital receipt.

  • ✓ Timestamped verification at every handover
  • ✓ Itemized weight and price audit records
  • ✓ Automated delivery confirmation SMS to recipient
04 / Measurable SME Outcomes

Restoring customer trust and unlocking repeat orders for small retailers.

-35% Dispatch Errors

Categorized package configuration cut fragile handling damages and misrouting.

4.8 / 5 Buyer Satisfaction

Direct driver communication and live map links turned delivery into a delight.

2.5x Repeat Purchases

Predictable delivery timelines restored shopper confidence in emerging online brands.
